# 💼 Complete Sales Guide — Selling the Sharda Chemistry Website

## 🎯 The Situation

| Factor | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Client** | Sharda Chemistry coaching, Begusarai, Bihar |
| **Owner** | Jitendra Kumar Sir (10+ yrs experience, respected locally) |
| **Market** | Small-town Bihar — zero competitors have websites |
| **Your Edge** | Website is ALREADY BUILT — you're not selling a promise, you're showing a product |
| **Google Rating** | 4.7★ with 360+ reviews — proves the business is legit |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Your biggest advantage: **The website is already done.** You're not asking him to imagine something — you're showing him his own brand looking world-class. This changes the entire psychology.

---

## 📋 Step-by-Step Game Plan

### Phase 1: Research (Before Meeting) — 30 mins

1. **Screenshot their Google Business Profile** — note down review count, rating, photos
2. **Check if ANY coaching in Begusarai has a website** — most won't. This is your ammo.
3. **Find their YouTube channel** (SHARDA CHEMISTRY) — note subscriber count
4. **Search "best chemistry coaching Begusarai" on Google** — show him he doesn't appear
5. **Save 3-4 competitor coaching names** — to show none have websites either

### Phase 2: The Approach

#### Option A: Walk-In (Best for small towns ✅)
- Visit the coaching center during **non-class hours** (2-4 PM or after 8 PM)
- Dress smart-casual (formal shirt, no suit — you want to look professional but relatable)
- Carry a **laptop with the website loaded** (keep it on localhost or deploy to Vercel first)
- Ask: *"Jitendra Sir se milna hai, 5 minute ka time chahiye"*

#### Option B: WhatsApp First
- Message on their WhatsApp number:

> *"Namaste Sir 🙏, Mera naam [Your Name] hai, main ek web developer hoon Begusarai se. Maine aapke coaching ka ek professional website banaya hai as a sample — jisme aapke courses, results, reviews sab hai. Kya aap 10 minute de sakte hain dekhne ke liye? Free hai dekhna, koi commitment nahi hai."*

> [!TIP]
> The magic words are **"already bana diya hai"** and **"free hai dekhna"**. This removes ALL risk from his side. He's just looking, not buying.

---

## 🗣️ The Sales Conversation Script

### Opening (2 minutes)

**You:** *"Sir namaste 🙏. Mera naam [Name] hai. Main web development karta hoon. Sir actually maine aapka Google profile dekha — 4.7 rating, 360+ reviews — Begusarai me itna koi coaching ka nahi hai. Bohot impressive hai.*

*Maine socha ki itni achi coaching ka ek world-class website hona chahiye. Toh maine ek sample bana diya. Ek baar dikha doon? Sirf 5 minute lagega."*

**Why this works:** You're leading with a COMPLIMENT about his achievement, not with a sales pitch. He feels respected.

### The Demo (5-7 minutes)

Open your laptop and show the website. **Let him see his own brand looking premium.**

Walk through each page while saying:

1. **Home Page:**
   *"Sir dekhiye — yeh aapka homepage hai. 5000+ students, 10+ years experience, 4.7 rating — sab dikhta hai. Koi bhi parent ya student jab Google pe search karega toh yeh dikhega."*

2. **Courses Page:**
   *"Yahan aapke saare courses hain — Class 11, 12, JEE, NEET — complete syllabus ke saath. Student ko pehle se pata chal jayega ki kya padhaya jayega."*

3. **Results Page:**
   *"Sir yeh sabse important page hai — aapke toppers, selections, reviews — sab ek jagah. Parents ke liye yahi sabse bada trust factor hai."*

4. **Contact Page (with WhatsApp form):**
   *"Sir yeh sabse powerful hai — student yahan form bharta hai, aur seedha aapke WhatsApp pe message aa jayega. Matlab 24/7 aapko enquiries milti rahegi — bina kisi phone call ke."*

5. **Mobile Demo:**
   *"Sir 90% students phone pe dekhenge — dekhiye mobile pe bhi kitna ache se kaam karta hai."*

> [!TIP]
> **Show it on your phone too.** Pull up the site on your phone and hand it to him. When he holds it in HIS hands and swipes through HIS coaching website — that's the emotional hook.

### The Pitch (3 minutes)

After the demo, pause. Let him react. Then:

**You:** *"Sir, aaj Begusarai me kisi bhi coaching ka website nahi hai. Matlab jo pehle bana lega, woh Google pe sabse pehle dikhega. Parents jab 'best chemistry coaching Begusarai' search karenge — sirf aapka naam aayega.*

*Ek achha website aapke liye kya karega:*

| Without Website | With Website |
|----------------|-------------|
| Students sirf word-of-mouth se aate hain | Google se bhi students aayenge |
| Parents ko trust karne me time lagta hai | Website dekhke pehle din se trust hota hai |
| Enquiry sirf call/visit se hoti hai | WhatsApp form se 24/7 enquiry aayegi |
| Koi online presence nahi | Professional brand image banti hai |
| Competitor kal website bana le toh peeche reh jaoge | Aap pehle hain, market leader dikhoge |

*Sir, aaj ek student ka admission fee ₹5,000-15,000 hai. Agar website se sirf 2-3 extra admission bhi ek saal me aaye — toh website ka paisa 10x wapas aa jayega."*

## 💰 Pricing Strategy

### Market Context
- Begusarai is a Tier-3 city in Bihar
- Monthly income for a coaching owner: ₹50K-2L
- No competitor has a website = no price reference point
- Students per batch: 50-200
- Annual fee per student: ₹5,000-15,000

### Recommended Pricing

| Package | Price | What's Included |
|---------|-------|-----------------|
| **Basic** | **₹8,000 - ₹10,000** | Website + Vercel deployment + 1 year hosting |
| **Standard** ⭐ | **₹12,000 - ₹15,000** | Website + Custom domain (.com) + SSL + Google Analytics + 1 year support |
| **Premium** | **₹18,000 - ₹20,000** | Everything above + Google Business optimization + SEO + monthly updates for 1 year |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Always quote the Standard package first at ₹15,000.** If he hesitates, drop to ₹12,000 or offer EMI (₹4,000 x 3 months). Never go below ₹8,000 — it devalues your work.

### How to Present the Price

**Don't say:** *"Sir ₹15,000 lagega"*

**Say:** *"Sir yeh website agar aap kisi company se banwate — Delhi ya Patna se — toh ₹40,000-₹80,000 lagta. Maine yeh already bana diya hai, toh aapke liye ₹15,000 me ready hai — domain, hosting, sab ke saath. Aur agar 2-3 extra admission bhi aaye saal me, toh ₹30,000-₹45,000 ka return hai. Matlab 2-3x return on investment."*

## 🛡️ Objection Handling

### "Humko website ki zaroorat nahi hai"

*"Sir samajh sakta hoon. 10 saal pehle logo ko WhatsApp ki bhi zaroorat nahi thi, aaj bina WhatsApp ke coaching nahi chal sakti. Website wahi stage pe hai — jo pehle adopt karega, woh aage rahega. Aur sir, parents check karte hain — especially jo thoda educated hain ya city se aaye hain."*

### "Bohot mehnga hai"

*"Sir, ₹12,000 matlab ₹1,000/month bhi nahi hai ek saal ke liye. Aapke ek student ki ek mahine ki fees se bhi kam hai. Aur agar isse sirf 1 extra admission bhi aaye toh — ₹10,000-₹15,000 ka direct return hai."*

*EMI option:* *"Sir agar ek baar me nahi dena toh 3 installment me de dijiye — ₹4,000 x 3 mahine."*

### "Koi dekhega bhi website ko?"

*"Sir, 'best coaching in Begusarai' Google pe mahine me 500+ baar search hota hai. Abhi kisi ka website nahi hai toh Google map result aata hai. Agar aapka website hoga toh aap #1 pe aayenge — because competition zero hai."*

### "Hum soch ke batate hain"

*"Bilkul sir, sochiye zaroor. Bas ek baat — abhi market me kisi coaching ka website nahi hai. Agar koi aur pehle bana le, toh Google pe woh pehle dikhega. Main 3-4 din wait kar sakta hoon, uske baad dusre coaching se baat karunga."*

> [!WARNING]
> The "I'll talk to another coaching" line creates FOMO (fear of missing out). Use it gently — don't threaten, just mention it casually.
